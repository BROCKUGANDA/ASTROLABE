    10→    10→## 1. Summary
    11→    11→
    12→    12→An employee joining Emirates or moving between roles, teams, or locations passes through dozens of steps owned by HR, IT, learning, facilities, immigration, payroll, and two managers. The steps exist; the map does not. Employees do not know what is next, who owns it, or whether the silence they are experiencing is normal. Problems surface as complaints and escalations, days after the signal was available.
    13→    13→
    14→    14→ASTROLABE is an employee-side AI companion that holds the journey map, knows exactly where the employee stands on it, answers policy questions with a citation to the governing document, starts and completes routine requests as complete correctly-routed packages, detects friction from events, request telemetry, and opt-in pulse feedback, and escalates to a named human owner with a ready-made summary when the rules say a human is needed.
    15→    15→
    16→    16→The thesis: journey intelligence is a **state and timing problem**, not a chat problem. Chat is the surface. A deterministic engine over an append-only event log is the product. That separation is what makes the companion auditable, defensible under HR scrutiny, and portable to any other government entity with a different journey map.
    17→    17→
    18→    18→## 2. Problem
    19→    19→
    20→    20→- A journey spans many owners and systems; nobody holds the end-to-end view, least of all the employee.
    21→    21→- The employee's default action when stuck is to wait, then complain. Both are expensive and late.
    22→    22→- Warning signals (a step past its target, a request bouncing between queues, a visa dependency frozen, a declining pulse score) exist in separate systems and never get correlated.
    23→    23→- Service owners receive incomplete requests, so the first action on most tickets is to ask the employee for something they were never told to bring.
    24→    24→- HR business partners discover stuck people through escalation channels, with no structured handoff and no history.
    25→    25→- An HR assistant that reads free-text comments and documents is an attack surface and a privacy surface at the same time. Both must be product features, not disclaimers.

    60→    60→## 5. Product principles
    61→    61→
    62→    62→1. **The engine decides, the model explains.** Bands, routes, and escalations come from rules over the event log. The model never emits a score.
    63→    63→2. **Every policy claim cites its source.** Policy answers render as quote + document + section + effective date, or they do not render.
    64→    64→3. **Unsure is a first-class answer.** Below the grounding threshold the companion says what it does not know and hands off to the named owner.
    65→    65→4. **For the employee, never on the employee.** No manager or HRBP surface exposes transcripts, sentiment text, or per-person pulse scores.
    66→    66→5. **Consent is a gate, not a banner.** Signal families are suppressed in computation when consent is absent, and the suppression is visible in the explanation.
    67→    67→6. **Propose, approve, act.** Every state change is typed, idempotent, audited, and confirmed by a human who owns it.
    68→    68→7. **Degrade loudly.** When retrieval or the model fails, say which layer is missing and still answer deterministically.
    69→    69→8. **Replayable or it did not happen.** Derived state is a pure function of the event log plus the journey map version.
    70→    70→9. **Bilingual by construction.** Arabic is a first-class locale with RTL layout and parallel policy citations, not a translation afterthought.
    71→    71→10. **Readable from the back of the room.** Projector legibility and keyboard-only operation are acceptance criteria.

   100→   100→## 8. Core journeys
   101→   101→
   102→   102→**E1 — "Where am I?" (primary, demoed).** Open companion → Today screen: stage chip (Onboarding · day 5), progress rail, next best step with owner and target date, late items in a Friction band card, one-tap actions.
   103→   103→
   104→   104→**E2 — Ask a policy question.** "How many days of relocation allowance do I get?" → staged progress (checking your journey → reading policy → drafting) → answer with inline citation chips → "View source" opens the exact policy section → follow-up suggestions → optional "start the request" button.
   105→   105→
   106→   106→**E3 — Start a routine request.** "My laptop hasn't arrived" → engine recognises the IT kit step is 3 days past target → prefilled intake (employee, location, stage, kit type, existing ticket reference) → completeness check → confirm preview → routed to the owning queue with idempotency key → status card on Requests.
   107→   107→
   108→   108→**E4 — Stuck dependency, early.** Visa step past target → engine raises DP-02 (weight 7, hard trip) → employee sees honest status, what it blocks, and what they can do now → companion asks consent to share summary with HRBP → escalation packet to named HRBP.
   109→   109→
   110→   110→**E5 — Opt-in pulse.** At a stage gate: "Two questions, only if you want to." Response stored with consent record; low confidence adds PS-01 to the employee's own friction view and (only if sharing consented) an aggregate signal to the HRBP queue, never the free text.
   111→   111→
   112→   112→**E6 — My data.** Me → Data held about me: every record, source, purpose, retention, who viewed it, with export and revoke.
   113→   113→
   114→   114→**H1 — HRBP queue.** Ranked by band and lead time: employee, stage, top three signals with evidence, suggested owner, claim button → claiming writes an audit event and notifies the employee that a human is on it.
   115→   115→
   116→   116→**H2 — Handoff.** Open packet → edit summary → assign to service owner or manager → due date → employee sees "HRBP Fatima is handling this".
   117→   117→
   118→   118→**M1v — Manager view.** My new joiners: steps **I** own, aggregate stage completion, no sentiment, no chat, no scores per person below the k-threshold.
   119→   119→
   120→   120→**S1 — Service owner intake.** Ticket arrives with complete payload, journey context, policy reference, and a one-click "needs more info" that returns a structured question to the employee rather than a free-text email.

   141→   141→## 10. Deterministic journey engine
   142→   142→
   143→   143→**Stage inference.** For employee `e` at time `T`: filter events to `occurred_at <= T`, map to steps via `journey_map`, compute per-step status (`not_started | in_progress | waiting_employee | waiting_owner | blocked | done | skipped`), then stage = the latest stage whose entry condition is satisfied and whose exit condition is not. Ties resolve to the earlier stage (conservative). Events for unknown steps raise DQ-05 and are listed as "unmapped activity", never dropped.
   144→   144→
   145→   145→**Next best step.** Candidates = steps in the current and next stage with status not done, dependencies satisfied, filtered by actor. Rank by: hard breach > blocks the most downstream steps > nearest due date > employee-actionable > shortest estimated effort. Return top 1 primary + up to 3 secondary, each with owner, target date, policy reference, and the action that starts it.
   146→   146→
   147→   147→**SLA clock.** `due_at = start + target_hours` adjusted by the location working calendar (weekend pattern, public holidays, employee leave if present). Breach levels: `none < due_soon (<20% remaining) < breached (>100%) < hard (>200%)`. Clock pauses while `waiting_employee` and that pause is shown to the employee, so the system never blames the employee silently or credits itself falsely.
   148→   148→
   149→   149→**Dependency graph.** Steps form a DAG from `depends_on`. Cycles are a config error that fails CI. Blocked steps report the root blocker, the chain length, and the count of downstream steps at risk — that count drives signal weight.
   150→   150→
   151→   151→**State machine (request).** `draft → validated → submitted → acknowledged → in_progress → waiting_employee ⇄ in_progress → resolved → closed`, with `returned` and `reassigned` as counted side-transitions and `cancelled` terminal. Illegal transitions are rejected with `E_CONFLICT` and logged. Every transition emits an event; status is never written directly.

   232→   232→## 13. Policy answering
   233→   233→
   234→   234→**Pipeline.** Normalise question → classify intent (policy | journey | action | smalltalk | out-of-scope) → retrieve: BM25 + vector hybrid over `policy_chunk`, locale-aware, reranked, top-k 8 → ground: the composer may use only retrieved chunk text + the employee's own journey facts → validate: every sentence containing a normative claim (amount, duration, eligibility, requirement) must map to a cited chunk ≥ similarity threshold → render answer with citation chips → log question, chunks, grounding score.
   235→   235→
   236→   236→**Contract.** Citation = document title + section + effective date + deep link to the rendered policy view with the quoted span highlighted. Conflicting policies: show both with effective dates, say which governs, and hand off if ambiguous. Policy older than its review date renders a staleness note. Unknown or unretrievable: abstain (M3) — never synthesise from general knowledge, never answer from the model's own HR knowledge.
   237→   237→
   238→   238→**Abstain triggers:** grounding score below threshold, top chunk below similarity floor, conflicting chunks with equal authority, question touches compensation/visa legal advice/medical/disciplinary, question is about another employee, or corpus version is unavailable.
   239→   239→
   240→   240→**Bilingual.** Answer in the question's language; cite the policy version in that language when it exists, otherwise cite the source language and say so. Arabic answers are composed from Arabic chunks when available, never machine-translated silently from English without a disclosure line.

   242→   242→## 14. Escalation and human oversight
   243→   243→
   244→   244→Escalation packet (typed, reviewable before send):
   245→   245→
   246→   246→- Employee, journey, stage, day-in-stage, locale.
   247→   247→- Band, score, and the three highest-weight signals with evidence references and timestamps.
   248→   248→- Timeline of the last 10 relevant events, with target vs actual.
   249→   249→- Blocked-downstream list and business impact (e.g. "blocks access, training, and badge").
   250→   250→- Open requests with owners, ages, and reassignment history.
   251→   251→- The decision requested, and the named owner with role and contact route.
   252→   252→- Consent statement: what the employee agreed to share, what is withheld, and whether free-text comments are excluded (they are, by default).
   253→   253→
   254→   254→Rules: every escalation has a named human owner (nullable owner is a schema violation); the employee is told an escalation exists and can see the packet contents about them; no escalation triggers any automated action on the employee; acknowledgement SLA 4h in-hours, with re-route to the backup owner on expiry; HRBP claim and close are audited.

   359→   359→
   360→   360→**Error taxonomy.** `E_VALIDATION` 400 · `E_AUTH` 401 · `E_FORBIDDEN` 403 · `E_NOT_FOUND` 404 · `E_CONFLICT` 409 (idempotency or illegal transition) · `E_CONSENT_REQUIRED` 412 · `E_UNPROCESSABLE` 422 (no named owner) · `E_RATE_LIMIT` 429 + `Retry-After` · `E_UPSTREAM_TIMEOUT` 504 · `E_DEGRADED` 503 with the degraded payload.    364→   364→## 20. Architecture, paths, and reliability
   365→   365→
   366→   366→**Path classes.**
   367→   367→
   368→   368→| Path | Contents | Budget | Fallback |
   369→   369→| --- | --- | --- | --- |
   370→   370→| Hot | Journey state, next step, band, cached policy answer, request list | p95 < 300ms, zero model calls | Serve from Redis, then from last assessment |
   371→   371→| Warm | Retrieval + compose, intake validation, packet generation | first token < 700ms, p95 < 2.5s | Extractive quote-only answer |
   372→   372→| Cold | Replay/backtest, analytics rollups, exports, embedding builds | minutes, queued | Resume from checkpoint |
   373→   373→
   374→   374→**Caching matrix.** Policy chunks: in-memory LRU 500 + Redis 24h, key `policy:{slug}:{corpusVersion}`. Embeddings: immutable by content hash in Postgres/pgvector, never invalidated, only versioned. Journey state: Redis 60s + SWR, invalidated on event write. Assessments: recomputed on event, memoised 30s. Semantic answer cache: key `sha256(normalisedQuestion|locale|corpusVersion|policyScope)`, TTL 1h, **bypassed** whenever the answer embeds employee-specific facts or consent state. Cached results always carry `servedFrom: cache` and an age.
   375→   375→
   376→   376→**Queues.** `events.ingest` (ordered per employee by partition key), `requests.dispatch`, `escalations.notify`, `analytics.rollup`, `replay.jobs`, each with a DLQ, visible depth, and an Admin requeue. Every handler is idempotent on `(jobKey, attempt-agnostic hash)`; at-least-once delivery is assumed.
   377→   377→
   378→   378→**Retries and backoff.**
   379→   379→
   380→   380→| Operation | Attempts | Base · factor · cap | Jitter | On exhaustion |
   381→   381→| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   382→   382→| Idempotent GET upstream | 3 | 200ms · 2 · 3s | full | Degrade to cache/deterministic |
   383→   383→| Model compose | 2 | 500ms · 2 · 4s | full | Extractive answer + banner |
   384→   384→| Retrieval query | 3 | 150ms · 2 · 1.5s | equal | Curated FAQ + handoff |
   385→   385→| Request dispatch | 5 | 2s · 2 · 5m | decorrelated | DLQ + employee sees "queued", HRBP notified |
   386→   386→| Notification / webhook | 6 | 1s · 3 · 2h | full | DLQ + in-app notice |
   387→   387→| DB write conflict | 3 | 50ms · 2 · 400ms | equal | 409 with safe retry hint |
   388→   388→
   389→   389→Never retry: non-idempotent POST without an idempotency key, 4xx other than 429/408, and anything with an expired deadline. Every call wraps in try/catch → typed error → `errorCode` metric → user-facing message with an action; swallowing an exception is a review-blocking defect. Circuit breaker per dependency: open at 50% failures over 20 calls, half-open probe after 30s, state exposed on `/readyz` and in the degraded banner. Deadline propagation: a request budget (3s hot, 8s warm) is carried in context and checked before each hop; bulkheads cap concurrent model calls (8) and retrieval calls (16) so one slow dependency cannot drain the pool.
   390→   390→
   391→   391→**Dedup.** Three layers: ingestion `(file_hash,row_hash)`; request creation `(employee,type,payload_hash,24h)`; answer logging `(question_hash,corpusVersion)` for cache stats. Pulse submissions dedupe per `(employee,stage,day)`.
   392→   392→
   393→   393→**Active-active.** Stateless app in two regions behind a geo load balancer; Postgres primary with read replica and connection-pooled failover; Redis per region with version-prefixed keys (no cross-region cache coherence needed because keys embed versions); event log is the single source of truth and all derived state is rebuildable by replay; writes are idempotent so retries across regions converge; no sticky sessions; all timestamps UTC. DR: RPO 5 minutes (PITR + streaming replica), RTO 15 minutes, restore drill rehearsed and timed in the cheatlist.
   394→   394→
   395→   395→**Observability.** Structured JSON logs with `requestId`/`traceId`/`employeeRef` (pseudonymous), OpenTelemetry traces across ask → retrieve → compose → validate, RED metrics per route, engine metrics (signals fired by code, band distribution, abstain rate, citation-validation drops, cache hit ratio, queue depth, DLQ size, breaker state). SLOs: hot availability 99.9%, ask p95 2.5s, zero uncited policy claims, escalation acknowledgement within 4h. Alerts page a human on breaker open, DLQ > 0, abstain rate > 25%, or audit-chain verification failure.
   396→   396→
   397→   397→**Feature flags / kill switches.** `ai_compose`, `semantic_cache`, `pulse_capture`, `auto_draft_escalation`, `manager_view`, `analytics_rollup`, `arabic_locale`, `judge_mode`. Each flips at runtime without deploy and is reflected in the UI banner.

   399→   399→## 21. Determinism and reproducibility
   400→   400→
   401→   401→Pinned toolchain and lockfile committed; model calls use temperature 0, fixed seed where supported, and pinned model IDs; all randomness seeded; engine functions are pure and property-tested; `map_version` + `corpus_version` + `engine_version` are recorded on every assessment and answer; a snapshot test suite of 20 golden assessments fails the build on any drift; the same event log and the same versions must reproduce byte-identical assessments on any machine.

   403→   403→## 22. Backtesting and evaluation harness
   404→   404→
   405→   405→**Replay-at-time.** The harness loads the synthetic pack as an append-only log, sets an as-of clock `T`, and recomputes assessments using only `occurred_at <= T` (and `recorded_at <= T` for systems that learned late). No future leakage; a leakage test asserts that assessments at `T` are invariant to events after `T`.
   406→   406→
   407→   407→**Datasets.** (a) full pack baseline; (b) 12 labelled scenarios with expected band, expected route, expected citation; (c) 8 adversarial cases: injection in a pulse comment, conflicting policies, missing consent, out-of-order events, duplicate requests, unknown policy topic, employee asking about a colleague, corrupted CSV row; (d) fairness slices across nationality/gender/tenure proxies; (e) load profile for latency.

   478→   478→| 48 | Employee record with PII beyond the schema | Dropped at ingestion, logged; never stored or sent to a model |

   490→   490→## 24. Failsafe modes
   491→   491→
   492→   492→| Level | Trigger | Behaviour | User-visible banner |
   493→   493→| --- | --- | --- | --- |
   494→   494→| L0 Full | All dependencies healthy | Everything on | none |
   495→   495→| L1 Extractive | Model provider failing or breaker open | Policy questions answered with direct quotes only, no synthesis; journey features untouched | "Quoting policy directly right now" |
   496→   496→| L2 Curated | Retrieval or index unavailable | Top-20 curated FAQ + handoff to named owners; policy answers otherwise abstain | "Policy search is unavailable — I'll hand you to the right owner" |
   497→   497→| L3 Queued writes | Downstream or queue degraded | Requests accepted and queued with a ticket reference; no success claimed until acknowledged | "Your request is queued, not lost" |
   498→   498→| L4 Safe read-only | DB primary failover or corpus/map invalid | Journey state from cache, owner directory, contact routes, consent view; all writes disabled | "Read-only mode — here is who to contact" |
   499→   499→
   500→   500→Rules: degradation is always announced, never silent; a degraded answer is never labelled as a live AI answer; `servedFrom` and `degraded[]` appear in the API envelope and the UI; recovery re-enables features automatically and tells the user what came back.

The short form of PRD — ASTROLABE v1.0 · Employee Journey Companion (Emirates challenge)ed 500 nword cheatsheet and checklis §29, in three depths: a 500-word summary, 50 checks carrying the condition that proves each one, then 500 individual implementation checks. Items 01–25 are functionality (does it do the job), 26–50 are production (will it survive contact with reality), and 001–500 are the granular engine, API, platform, and release checks you tick while building.

## The 500-word version

The whole product in one read: what it must do, and what must be true for it to survive production.

**Functionality**

- **State.** The journey map is versioned config, not code. Stage, step status, and band derive only from an append-only event log. Delete every derived table, replay, and get identical state back.
- **Direction.** Today shows one next step with its owner, due date, and whose turn it is. Blockers read as sentences. Clocks pause visibly when the employee is the holdup, and the pause is explained.
- **Answers.** A policy claim renders with document, section, and effective date, or it does not render. Amounts and deadlines are quoted verbatim. Conflicts show both sources and name the one that governs.
- **Honesty.** Below the grounding threshold, or on pay, legal, medical, and disciplinary topics, it abstains, names a human, and prefills the question. Unsure beats confident and wrong, every time.
- **Requests.** Intake prefills from journey state, blocks submit until the type schema validates, previews the exact payload and recipient, and returns the same ticket on resubmission rather than a second queue entry.
- **Routing.** Owner resolution always ends somewhere nameable: step owner, location queue, function, HRBP, or an explicit admission of who was told instead. Status changes only through legal transitions, each emitting an event.
- **Detection.** Forty-two signals over events, request telemetry, and consented pulse produce four bands with evidence attached. Rules own the verdict; the model explains, drafts, and translates, and never scores.
- **Boundaries.** Consent gates computation and the suppression is disclosed. Managers see owned steps and aggregates only, enforced in the response schema. Escalations require a named human and a one-screen packet.

**Production**

- **Paths.** The hot path serves journey state and cached answers under 300ms with zero model calls. Warm streams a first token under 700ms. Cold work is queued, checkpointed, and rate-capped.
- **Resilience.** Timeouts and deadline budgets on every hop, jittered exponential backoff with bounded attempts, circuit breakers with visible state, bulkheads per dependency, and dead-letter queues an operator can inspect and requeue.
- **Idempotency.** Keys on every create and transition, dedup at ingestion, creation, and notification, version-stamped cache keys, and no shared cache for employee-specific or consent-dependent answers.
- **Degradation.** Four announced levels: extractive quotes, curated fallback, queued writes, read-only safe mode. Nothing cached or extractive is ever dressed up as a live AI answer.
- **Safety.** Untrusted text travels as data to a model with no tools, output is schema-validated before render, and injection attempts become findings. Secrets stay server-side and the bundle is scanned.
- **Accountability.** Append-only hash-chained audit verified in CI, row-level security, a purpose claim on every sensitive read, tested retention jobs, and an employee-visible log of who looked.
- **Operability.** Runtime kill switches, structured logs and traces keyed by request id, service levels that page a named owner with a runbook, one-command deploy and rollback, and a drilled recovery.
- **Proof.** Merge-blocking gates: routing accuracy, recall, precision, citation presence, abstain correctness, injection resistance, latency, byte-identical replay. Freeze a day out, rehearse twice, keep an offline recording.

If a check has no condition that proves it, it is not a check. Everything above is demonstrable live, or it does not ship.

## A. Functionality (01–25)

- [ ]  **01 · Journey state is derived, never stored.** Stage, step status, and band come only from the append-only event log plus the versioned map. No hand-written status columns, no admin overrides. Done when: deleting every derived table and replaying the log rebuilds byte-identical state, proven three runs in a row.
- [ ]  **02 · Next best step is exactly one thing.** The engine ranks candidates by hard breach, downstream blocked count, nearest due date, and employee-actionability, then shows one primary action with at most three secondary. Done when: a user on Today can say out loud what to do next within ten seconds.
- [ ]  **03 · Whose turn it is, stated plainly.** Every step names you, a person, or a team, with a contact route. No orphan states, no "in progress" without an actor. Done when: no screen in the demo shows a pending item whose owner a judge cannot read off the card.
- [ ]  **04 · The SLA clock is honest in both directions.** Clocks adjust to the location calendar and pause while waiting on the employee, with the pause visible. The system never blames the employee silently, nor credits itself falsely. Done when: a paused item shows the reason and no breach is counted during the pause.
- [ ]  **05 · Blockers explained in sentences, not graphs.** The chain reads "badge waits on visa, which waits on your passport scan," with the root blocker and the count of downstream steps at risk. Done when: a non-technical tester can restate the blocker correctly after one read, without the diagram.
- [ ]  **06 · A policy claim renders with a citation or not at all.** Document, section, effective date, and a deep link to the highlighted span. Amounts and deadlines are quoted verbatim. Done when: the grounding validator drops every unanchored sentence and the counter of dropped claims is visible in telemetry.
- [ ]  **07 · Abstain names a human and prefills the question.** Below the grounding threshold, or on compensation, legal, medical, and disciplinary topics, the companion says it is unsure and hands off. Done when: abstains read as a handoff, never a failure, and abstain correctness measures at or above 0.95.
- [ ]  **08 · Conflicting policies both appear.** Show both documents with effective dates, name the one that governs, and hand off when authority is equal. Never silently pick the top retrieval hit. Done when: the seeded conflict case renders both sources and the governing line, verified in the backtest.
- [ ]  **09 · Bilingual answers disclose their source language.** Answer in the language asked, cite same-language chunks when they exist, and state plainly when an Arabic answer rests on an English-only policy. Done when: the Arabic demo path shows a correct citation, correct RTL layout, and the disclosure line.
- [ ]  **10 · Intake is prefilled from journey state and labelled as such.** Employee, location, stage, dates, prior ticket, and policy reference arrive already filled, marked prefilled, and editable. Done when: no request type asks for a fact the system already holds, checked against the data dictionary field by field.
- [ ]  **11 · Submission is blocked until the type schema validates.** Required fields per request type, inline specific errors, and a plain statement of what is missing. Done when: every one of the eight types produces a ticket that needs zero clarification round-trips in the harness run.
- [ ]  **12 · The confirm step shows exactly what is sent and to whom.** Full payload, named recipient queue, expected-by date. No hidden attachments, no surprise sharing of pulse data. Done when: a judge can read the preview, compare it to the dispatched payload in the trace, and find them identical.
- [ ]  **13 · Duplicate submissions return the existing ticket.** Idempotency on employee, type, payload hash, and a 24-hour window, with the duplicate signal raised for the HRBP view rather than a second queue entry. Done when: double-tapping submit, or resubmitting after reconnect, yields one ticket and one notification.
- [ ]  **14 · Owner resolution always terminates somewhere nameable.** Step owner, then location queue, then functional fallback, then HRBP, then an explicit "no owner found, here is who I told instead." Done when: no request can reach a null owner, and the fallback path is exercised by a test with deliberately missing mappings.
- [ ]  **15 · Request status is derived and transitions are legal-only.** Every move emits an event; illegal transitions return a conflict and are logged; reassignment and return counts are tracked and shown. Done when: status cannot be written directly anywhere in the codebase, verified by a grep and a failing-write test.
- [ ]  **16 · Every friction signal returns evidence, never prose.** Signals carry ids, weights, and references to the events or requests that fired them, so the band explains itself. Done when: the engine trace lists each fired signal with its evidence, and no band can be produced without at least one reference.
- [ ]  **17 · Hard trips cannot be outvoted by a low total.** A stalled visa dependency, an explicit help-now flag, or an unconfirmed payroll change before cutoff forces the Blocked band regardless of arithmetic. Done when: property tests prove no combination of low-weight signals can suppress a hard trip.
- [ ]  **18 · Consent gates computation and the gate is disclosed.** Unconsented families contribute zero inside the engine, not merely hidden in the UI, and the explanation says which inputs were excluded. Done when: toggling pulse consent live recomputes the band on screen and names the suppressed family.
- [ ]  **19 · Escalation requires a named human and a one-screen packet.** Situation, band, top three signals with evidence, timeline of target versus actual, blocked impact, open requests, and the decision requested. Done when: the schema rejects a null owner with a 422 and the UI cannot submit one.
- [ ]  **20 · The employee sees the packet written about them.** Including what was shared, what was withheld, and that free text is excluded by default. They can add a sentence or decline. Done when: the employee view and the HRBP view of the same case reconcile field by field.
- [ ]  **21 · Manager surfaces cannot return sentiment, by schema.** No pulse scores, comments, transcripts, or per-person friction in the response type, not merely absent from the component. Done when: the network payload for the manager route is inspected live and provably contains only owned steps and aggregates.
- [ ]  **22 · Aggregates suppress below the k threshold.** Any cohort cell with fewer than five people renders "not enough data to show safely," enforced server-side so no client request can widen it. Done when: a filter deliberately narrowed to two employees returns suppression, not numbers.
- [ ]  **23 · "Everything I know about you" is a real screen.** Each record with source, purpose, retention, and a log of who viewed it, plus export and one-tap revoke with a receipt. Done when: an employee can answer what is held, why, and who looked, without contacting support.
- [ ]  **24 · Any state can be constructed for a hidden scenario.** Admin can seed arbitrary events, set an as-of clock, and replay, including contradictory states like a step completed before its prerequisite. Done when: three states nobody designed for produce a safe, explained, correctly routed answer with no crash.
- [ ]  **25 · The deterministic core works with the model switched off.** Journey state, next step, bands, routing, owner resolution, and verbatim policy quotes all survive. Done when: the full demo is walked end to end with the provider disabled and only the composition layer degrades, loudly.

## B. Production (26–50)

- [ ]  **26 · Pinned stack, committed lockfile, no silent drift.** Runtime, framework, model ids, and thresholds are versioned config, recorded on every assessment and answer. Done when: a clean clone on another machine reproduces identical golden snapshots, and any weight or band change requires a written change note.
- [ ]  **27 · One response envelope, typed errors, abstain is not an error.** Every route returns the same shape with request id, servedFrom, degraded flags, and versions. Done when: contract tests cover every error code, and the abstain payload arrives as a 200 with a reason code and owner hint.
- [ ]  **28 · Idempotency keys on every create and transition.** Replays return the existing resource with a flag rather than a second side effect, across retries, reconnects, and region failover. Done when: firing the same create ten times concurrently produces exactly one ticket and one notification.
- [ ]  **29 · Timeouts and deadline budgets on every hop.** Three seconds hot, eight warm, carried in context and checked before each call so doomed work is abandoned instead of queued. Done when: no handler can exceed its budget, proven by a fault-injection test that slows each dependency in turn.
- [ ]  **30 · Exponential backoff with jitter, bounded attempts, no blind loops.** Per-operation policies with defined exhaustion behaviour, and never a retry of a non-idempotent write without a key. Done when: retry counts, delays, and final outcomes appear in traces for each dependency class under induced failure.
- [ ]  **31 · Circuit breakers with visible state.** Open at half failures over twenty calls, half-open probe at thirty seconds, state exposed on readiness and reflected in the user-facing banner. Done when: killing a dependency flips the breaker, the banner names the missing layer, and recovery is announced.
- [ ]  **32 · Bulkheads cap concurrency per dependency.** Model and retrieval calls have separate pools so one slow provider cannot drain the process and take the hot path with it. Done when: a load test with a deliberately slow model leaves journey state responses inside their latency budget.
- [ ]  **33 · Queues have dead-letter paths and an operator can requeue.** Depth, age, and failure reason are visible; exhaustion notifies a human while the employee sees "queued, not lost." Done when: a poisoned job lands in the dead-letter queue, raises an alert, and replays cleanly after a fix.
- [ ]  **34 · Dedup at ingestion, creation, and notification.** File and row hashes on load, payload hashes on requests, stable keys on messages, so at-least-once delivery never becomes at-least-twice action. Done when: re-ingesting the pack adds no rows and a notification storm of fifty events sends one digest.
- [ ]  **35 · Cache keys carry versions and sensitive answers bypass the cache.** Map and corpus versions are in the key; employee-specific or consent-dependent answers are never served from a shared cache. Done when: bumping the corpus version invalidates cleanly and no cached answer leaks another person's facts.
- [ ]  **36 · The hot path makes zero model calls.** Journey state, next step, band, request list, and cached policy answers serve inside a 300ms budget, independent of any provider. Done when: the p95 holds on conference wifi with the model provider turned off entirely.
- [ ]  **37 · Degradation is a ladder, and it is announced.** Extractive quotes, curated fallback, queued writes, read-only safe mode, each with honest copy naming what is missing. Done when: a chaos drill walks all four levels and no degraded output is ever presented as a live AI answer.
- [ ]  **38 · Kill switches flip without a deploy.** Compose, semantic cache, pulse capture, auto-draft escalation, manager view, analytics, locale, judge mode, all runtime flags reflected in the interface. Done when: each switch is exercised during rehearsal and the resulting user-visible state is checked and documented.
- [ ]  **39 · The audit log is append-only and hash-chained.** Every sensitive read, answer, route, escalation, and consent change is recorded with actor and lawful basis, verified by a job in continuous integration. Done when: a tamper test mutates one row and the verification job fails loudly.
- [ ]  **40 · Row-level security plus a purpose claim on sensitive reads.** Access is enforced in the data layer, not the component tree, and reading pulse data without a declared purpose is impossible. Done when: negative tests prove one employee cannot read another's record through any route or parameter.
- [ ]  **41 · Untrusted text is data, model output is a schema.** Policies, comments, and tickets travel in fenced envelopes to a model with no tools, and output is schema-validated before anything renders. Done when: twenty injection payloads change no band, owner, route, or citation, and each is logged.
- [ ]  **42 · Secrets stay server-side and the bundle is scanned.** No provider key in a public variable, least-privilege credentials, strict content security policy, signed short-lived sessions. Done when: the build fails on a detected key pattern and a reviewer can point to where every secret is read.
- [ ]  **43 · Structured logs and traces carry the request id end to end.** Ask, retrieve, compose, validate, and dispatch appear as one trace with a pseudonymous subject and no personal data in the payload. Done when: a support query resolves from a single copied request id in under a minute.
- [ ]  **44 · Service levels with alerts that page a named human.** Hot availability, ask latency, zero uncited claims, four-hour escalation acknowledgement, each with a threshold, a runbook link, and an owner. Done when: every alert that can fire has been fired once in staging and the runbook worked.
- [ ]  **45 · Backtest gates block the merge.** Routing accuracy, friction recall and precision, citation presence, abstain correctness, false escalation rate, injection resistance, and latency run on every pull request. Done when: a deliberately weakened threshold is caught by the pipeline rather than by a judge.
- [ ]  **46 · Replay is deterministic and leak-free.** Assessments at a given as-of clock are invariant to later events, and three runs produce identical bytes on any machine. Done when: the leakage assertion fails if a future event is accidentally included, and the snapshot diff is empty.
- [ ]  **47 · Migrations are forward-only and the seed is fast.** Reviewed schema changes with a documented recovery plan, and a script that rebuilds the full demo state, including scenarios, in under two minutes. Done when: a fresh environment is demo-ready from a single command, timed and recorded.
- [ ]  **48 · One command deploys, one command rolls back.** Previous build pinned and tested, preview environment per pull request with seeded data, configuration from environment only. Done when: a rollback is performed for real during rehearsal and the previous version serves traffic within two minutes.
- [ ]  **49 · Disaster recovery is drilled, not asserted.** Point-in-time recovery and a replica, with a five-minute recovery point and fifteen-minute recovery time objective, plus derived state rebuilt by replay. Done when: a timed restore meets both targets and the resulting assessments match the pre-failure snapshots.
- [ ]  **50 · Freeze, rehearse, and keep an offline recording.** Code frozen a day out, copy and configuration only after that, demo run end to end twice including the degraded path, recording tested on the venue machine. Done when: the whole demo can be delivered with no network at all.
